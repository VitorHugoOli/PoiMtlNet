\chapter{Other Scientific Contributions}
\label{apx:contributions}

Besides the three articles that form Chapters~\ref{ch:cbic} to~\ref{ch:mobiwac}, one
additional manuscript was produced during this research. In the first half of 2026,
between the study of Chapter~\ref{ch:courb} and the consolidated study of
Chapter~\ref{ch:mobiwac}, an earlier iteration of the check-in-level line of work was
submitted to the Brazilian Conference on Intelligent Systems (BRACIS 2026) under the
title \emph{Substrate Carries, Architecture Pays: Check-In-Level Embeddings for
Multi-Task POI Prediction}. The submission was rejected, with notification on June 8,
2026, and remains unpublished.

The manuscript is an earlier unpublished iteration of the study presented in
Chapter~\ref{ch:mobiwac}. It introduced a first version of the check-in-level
representation, evaluated under an older protocol and an earlier training
configuration, and its central claim, that multi-task learning imposes a cost on
region prediction, was later corrected by the MobiWac study: the observed cost traced
back to a numerical-precision artifact of that training configuration and to the older
evaluation protocol. For this reason, no result of the manuscript is cited as evidence
anywhere in this dissertation, and its conclusions are superseded in full by
Chapter~\ref{ch:mobiwac}.

The material of the submission was absorbed into the study of
Chapter~\ref{ch:mobiwac}, which redesigned the evaluation before any claim was
reissued. The reviewers' principal objection to the manuscript, the possibility of
label leakage through the transductive representation, is answered there by a
dedicated leakage audit under the user-disjoint protocol
(Chapter~\ref{ch:mobiwac}).
