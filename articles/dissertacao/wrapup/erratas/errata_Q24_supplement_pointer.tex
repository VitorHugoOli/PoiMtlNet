\paragraph*{Errata: o apontamento para o volume suplementar (secao 1.5).}
Ao fim da secao de organizacao, acrescenta-se:

\begin{quote}
A companion volume, published beside this one, holds five appendices: an errata recording every
departure of the three reproduced chapters from their published or submitted originals; a
label-history benchmark for the next-category task; the author's position on research-ethics review;
the selection of one hyperparameter of the place-embedding baseline; and a parameter-count control
for the dedicated next-category model. This volume does not cross-reference them individually,
because a reference cannot resolve across the two documents.
\end{quote}

\noindent
Sem essa frase, um leitor do volume principal nao tem indicacao de que os cinco apendices existem.
