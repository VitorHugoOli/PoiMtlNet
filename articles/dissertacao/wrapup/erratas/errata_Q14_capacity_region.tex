\paragraph*{Errata: o limite de capacidade no eixo de regiao (Cap.~5, lista de limites).}
A lista passa a ter cinco membros, e o quinto le:

\begin{quote}
Capacity on the region axis. The joint model carries more parameters than the two dedicated
models combined, so its region result at Texas and California is confounded with capacity. A
capacity-matched control separates the two: giving the dedicated region model the joint model's
entire parameter budget raises it above the joint model at California, by $0.43$ Acc@10 with the
difference separated from zero over five folds ($p = 0.008$, unanimous in direction), and to
within $0.21$ Acc@10 at Texas, where the difference is not separated from zero ($p = 0.12$). A
narrower arm at $57$ percent of that budget already reaches the same level, and the step from it
to the full budget is not separated from zero. The region advantage reported here therefore
measures capacity rather than transfer between the tasks, and the claim that survives is the
operational one: one model produces both predictions in one forward pass at no measured cost on
either task. The category axis is unaffected: there, a control that multiplies the dedicated
model's parameters by $6.5$ lowers its macro-F1.
\end{quote}

\noindent
Esta errata enfraquece uma afirmacao do texto depositado. O custo de parametros ja estava
reportado (p.~73) e a atribuicao do resultado de regiao ja nao creditava o tronco compartilhado
(p.~84); o que faltava era o limite nomeado e, agora, a medicao que o fecha.
