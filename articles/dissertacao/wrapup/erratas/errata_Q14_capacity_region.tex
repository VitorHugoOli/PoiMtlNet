\paragraph*{Errata: o limite de capacidade no eixo de regiao (Cap.~5, lista de limites).}
A lista passa a ter cinco membros. O quinto le:

\begin{quote}
Capacity on the region axis. The joint model has more parameters than the two dedicated models
together, so its region result at Texas and California may come from size rather than from sharing.
A control separates the two. It gives the dedicated region model the joint model's entire parameter
budget and changes nothing else. At California the wider dedicated model then scores above the
joint model, by $0.43$ Acc@10, and a paired test over the five folds separates that difference from
zero ($p = 0.008$), with all five folds in the same direction. At Texas the two are $0.21$ Acc@10
apart, which the same test does not separate from zero ($p = 0.12$). A narrower model, with $57$
percent of that budget, already reaches the same level. The region advantage reported here therefore
measures capacity, not transfer between the tasks. What remains is the operational result: one model
produces both predictions in one forward pass, at no measured cost on either task. The category axis
is not affected, because there a dedicated model with $6.5$ times the parameters scores lower, not
higher.
\end{quote}

\noindent
Esta errata enfraquece uma afirmacao do texto depositado. O custo de parametros ja aparece na
p.~73, e a p.~84 ja nao credita o resultado de regiao ao tronco compartilhado. O que faltava era o
limite nomeado e, agora, a medicao que o fecha.
