\paragraph*{Errata: o escopo do controle de concatenacao (Cap.~5, p.~79).}
A frase corrigida le:

\begin{quote}
Adding the per-visit features to the place embedding raises it by $+2.0$, $+1.7$, and $+0.8$
macro-F1 at Alabama, Arizona, and Florida. Those numbers come from a separate study, which used a
different version of the graph and its own training code. They are not on the scale of Table~9 and
are not placed beside it. This control therefore does not separate the hierarchical representation
from the per-visit features that it reads, and no such separation is claimed here. The evidence
that the representation is the dominant factor is Table~9, where the input is the only thing that
changes.
\end{quote}

\noindent
A frase substituida afirmava que o ganho vem da representacao hierarquica e nao de injecao de
features. Os tres numeros estao corretos. O que falta na frase depositada e dizer a que escala eles
pertencem.
