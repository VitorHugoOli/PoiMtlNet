\paragraph*{Errata: o escopo do controle de concatenacao (Cap.~5, p.~79).}
A frase depositada le, na sua forma corrigida:

\begin{quote}
Concatenating the per-visit features onto the place embedding raises it by $+2.0$, $+1.7$, and
$+0.8$ macro-F1 at Alabama, Arizona, and Florida. That control belongs to a separate study: a
different variant of the graph, its own harness, and embeddings built on different hardware, so
its values are not on the scale of Table~9 and are not placed beside it. Within that study the
margins are the size of the category differences reported here or larger. On the category axis,
therefore, this control does not separate the hierarchical per-visit representation from the
per-visit features it reads, and no such separation is claimed. The evidence that the
representation is the dominant factor rests on Table~9, where the input is the only quantity
that changes.
\end{quote}

\noindent
A frase substituida afirmava que o ganho vem da representacao hierarquica e nao de injecao de
features. A fracao que a sustentava e calculada contra o intervalo interno daquele estudo, nao
contra a Tabela~9, e na escala da Tabela~9 ela nao se sustenta.
