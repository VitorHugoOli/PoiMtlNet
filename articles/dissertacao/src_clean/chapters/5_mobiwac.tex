\chapter[A Check-in-Level Multitask Study of Next Category and Region]{A Check-in-Level Multitask Study of Next Category and Region}
\label{ch:mobiwac}

\begin{chapterpreface}
This chapter reproduces the article ``Predicting the Next Category and Region of a
Visit: A Check-in-Level Multi-Task Study on Mobility Data,'' submitted to MobiWac
2026, under review at the time of writing (EDAS \#1571313639), in the revised form
that followed the final evaluation. It closes the
investigation that Chapters~\ref{ch:cbic} and~\ref{ch:courb} open: with a
check-in-level representation and a redesigned sharing topology, a single joint
model replaces two dedicated single-task
models across the six datasets studied (five U.S. states and Istanbul), and it
outperforms them on next region at the two datasets with the largest region
vocabularies and on next category at one dataset. The article was
reformatted from its two-column original into the dissertation layout: sections,
figures, and tables were renumbered, and the figures were regenerated at
single-column width.
\end{chapterpreface}
